# Vizibil în Mișcare — Brand v0.2

## Role

Romania-first validation brand for a marketplace that connects local businesses with moving physical advertising inventory.

## Domain

`vizibilinmiscare.ro`

## Positioning

**Descriptor:** Publicitate locală pe trasee reale.

**Business promise:** Fă-ți afacerea vizibilă pe traseele pe care clienții tăi le parcurg deja.

**Driver promise:** Fă bani din drumurile pe care le faci deja.

**Proof line:** Trasee reale. Preț clar. Campanii verificate.

## Brand architecture

The name is deliberately broader than cars. Cars are the first inventory type, not necessarily the final category.

Do not describe the company as a traditional advertising agency. The product is a marketplace / booking-and-verification layer.

## Visual system

- Deep Ink: `#1F1830`
- Signal Lime: `#C7F36B`
- Bone: `#F5F2EA`
- Slate: `#2D3138`
- Route Violet: `#7C72F2`

Typography:
- Manrope — brand/headings
- Inter — body/UI

## Logo

Primary identity is a text wordmark: `Vizibil în Mișcare` with a restrained Signal Lime underline under `Vizibil`.

The compact app/favicon mark uses an abstract moving route between two nodes.

Do not add:
- car silhouette;
- steering wheel;
- map pin;
- megaphone;
- speedometer;
- orange accents;
- generic SaaS blue gradient.

## Tone

Direct, practical, measurable, local. Avoid agency jargon and speculative performance claims.

## Validation disclosure

Synthetic marketplace inventory must remain explicitly labelled as demo/sample inventory until replaced with qualified real vehicles.
